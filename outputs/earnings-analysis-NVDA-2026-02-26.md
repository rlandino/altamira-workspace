## 5. Earnings call — prepared remarks highlights

- **Record quarter:** Revenue $68B (+73% y/y), record operating income and free cash flow. Data center +$11B sequentially; full-year data center $194B (+68% y/y).
- **Outlook:** Sequential revenue growth expected throughout calendar 2026; Q1 FY27 revenue guide $78B (±2%). Supply/inventory commitments extend into calendar 2027.
- **Data center:** Q4 $62B (+75% y/y, +22% seq). Blackwell and Blackwell Ultra ramp; Hopper and much of Ampere still sold out in cloud. Networking $11B in Q4 (>3.5x y/y); full-year networking >$31B.
- **Agentic AI inflection:** Management framed agentic AI (Codex, Claude Cowork, OpenClaw) at an inflection; “compute equals revenues” for CSPs; token monetization driving urgency to scale compute.
- **Segments:** Gaming $3.7B (+47% y/y); Pro Viz crossed $1B ($1.3B, +159% y/y); Automotive $604M (+6% y/y). Gaming supply expected to remain a headwind in Q1 and beyond.
- **Margins:** Non-GAAP gross margin 75.2%; guide for Q1 ~75% (±50 bps). Full-year gross margins expected mid-70s.
- **Capital return:** FY26 returned $41B (43% of FCF) via buybacks and dividends. Stock-based comp to be included in non-GAAP from Q1 FY27.
- **Rubin:** Vera Rubin samples shipped; production in second half of calendar year. Rubin platform (Vera CPU, Rubin GPU, NVLink 6, Spectrum-6, etc.) aimed at MOE training and lower inference token cost vs Blackwell.

## 7. Sentiment analysis (management answers)

- **Tone:** **Confident.** Repeated framing of “compute equals revenues,” inflection in agentic AI, and leadership in performance per watt. No defensive or apologetic language; clear forward commitment to roadmap (Blackwell, Rubin) and ecosystem investments.
- **Sentiment:** **Positive.** Optimism on CapEx sustainability, customer cash flow, sequential growth through the year, and long-term data center opportunity. Cautious only on Gaming supply and China revenue (no assumption in outlook).
- **Clarity:** **Clear and specific.** Numerical guidance (Q1 $78B ±2%, gross margin ~75%, OpEx), segment breakdowns, and explicit reiteration of prior frameworks ($3–4T CapEx, “compute = revenues”). Few evasive answers; “more at GTC” used for product/architecture detail.
- **Notable Q&A themes:** (1) **Cloud CapEx and sustainability** — Management tied CapEx to token monetization and agentic AI inflection. (2) **Ecosystem and strategic investments** — Balance sheet used to deepen ecosystem and platform adoption. (3) **Margins and innovation** — Gross margin sustainability linked to generational performance leads. (4) **Supply and segments** — Gaming supply tight; Data Center and Networking strength emphasized.
