## Metrics to monitor

- **Revenue and order cycle:** Semiconductor capex cycle; EUV and DUV system demand; China export controls and geographic mix.
- **FCF margin sustainability:** High margin (32.6%) with modest YoY decline; working capital and CapEx phasing.
- **ROIC vs cycle:** ROIC 35.5% is strong; monitor through down-cycles (e.g. FY24 softness).
- **EPS and capital allocation:** Buybacks and dividends; balance of growth CapEx vs shareholder returns.
- **Competitive moat:** EUV monopoly and technology roadmap; customer concentration and long-term agreements.
- **Geopolitical and export:** US/Netherlands export rules; China exposure and mitigation.
