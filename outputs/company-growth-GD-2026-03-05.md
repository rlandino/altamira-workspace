## Metrics to monitor

- **Revenue durability:** Defense and aerospace backlog support growth; watch Aerospace (Gulfstream) and Marine/Combat for order flow and mix.
- **FCF margin sustainability:** FCF margin recovered to 7.5% in FY25; monitor working capital and CapEx for consistency.
- **ROIC vs cost of capital:** ROIC at 10.6% is above typical cost of capital; track incremental returns on new programs.
- **EPS consistency vs consensus:** Solid FY25 EPS growth (13.4% YoY); monitor execution and share repurchases vs expectations.
- **Rule of 40:** Currently Solid (20.3); small gains in revenue growth or operating margin would maintain tier.
- **Program execution:** Defense contracting margins and cash conversion depend on program performance and contract structure.
