## Why the Market is Moving

Markets rallied strongly on **geopolitical relief and diplomatic progress**: Trump's announcement of a framework agreement with Iran and cancellation of strike plans significantly reduced Middle East tension and oil price risk. Oil fell below $90/barrel as sanctions lift potential and Hormuz strait reopening prospects improved, removing a key macro headwind. The move reinforces risk-on sentiment amid **broader central bank pressure concerns**—the Fed and peers face inflation/growth trade-offs, but easing geopolitical fears bought the tape time. Tech led (+2.54% Nasdaq vs +1.75% S&P 500) as chip stocks rebounded after a volatile week, and the VIX compression to 18.84 (normalized from 19.44) signals lower hedging demand in this window.

## Summary

Markets closed higher across all three major indices on Iran deal optimism and reduced oil/geopolitical tail risk. Risk appetite returned with VIX compression; Nasdaq outperformed on tech recovery. Central banks remain in focus, but the immediate catalyst (Iran tensions) has eased, supporting equities.
