## 4. Metrics to monitor

- **Revenue deceleration:** Watch for any slowdown in semiconductor and infrastructure software growth; AVGO’s revenue trajectory is currently accelerating.
- **FCF margin sustainability:** 44% FCF margin is high; monitor that it holds as mix and capex evolve.
- **ROIC vs cost of capital:** ROIC at 16.7% is above typical cost of capital; track that it remains so after VMware integration and segment shifts.
- **EPS consistency vs consensus:** Latest YoY is very strong; monitor future quarters for continued beats or in-line results.
- **Rule of 40 trend:** Currently Elite (63.8); monitor that the score stays in Elite or Solid range as growth normalizes.
- **Integration and guidance:** VMware and portfolio changes can affect margins and growth; follow management guidance and segment disclosure.
