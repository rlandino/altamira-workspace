**Rating:** **Wide**

| Source | Score (1–5) | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Brand power** | 5 | Microsoft, Azure, Office, and Windows are default choices for enterprises and consumers globally. |

**Summary:** Wide moat from multiple durable advantages: enterprise lock-in, cloud scale, and ecosystem. Main risks are regulatory and competitive (AI, cloud).

## Margin of Safety

- **Current price:** $408.96  
- **Base-case intrinsic value:** $286  

**Discount to base case:** ($286 − $408.96) ÷ $286 ≈ **−43%** → stock trades at about a **43% premium** to base-case intrinsic value.

- Versus bear ($260): large premium.  
- Versus bull ($436): about **6% below** bull-case intrinsic value.

**Buffett 30% rule:** Price is **not** 30%+ below intrinsic value. It is above base and bear; only slightly below bull. **No margin of safety** on base case.

---

## Verdict

**Hold** — Wide-moat, high-quality franchise, but current price implies no margin of safety versus base (43% premium). Bull case supports the quoted price; base and bear do not. For a value-oriented entry, wait for a meaningful discount to base-case intrinsic value (e.g. 30%+ below $286, or ~$200 and below).

## Top 3 Permanent Impairment Risks

1. **Regulatory and antitrust** — Broader antitrust and cloud/software regulation could force divestitures, limit bundling, or constrain pricing and integration in ways that permanently reduce returns and moat.  
2. **AI and cloud competition** — Shift in share to Google, Amazon, or others in AI and cloud could compress growth and margins and narrow the moat if Microsoft falls behind in key workloads.  
3. **Leadership and execution** — Heavy reliance on Nadella-era strategy and culture; a poorly executed succession or strategic misstep could erode execution premium and investor confidence.
