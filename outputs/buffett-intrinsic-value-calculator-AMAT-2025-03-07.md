| Source | Score (1–5) | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Brand power** | 4 | Leading brand in semiconductor manufacturing equipment; customers specify AMAT in fabs. |

## Margin of Safety

- **Current price:** $324.74  
- **Base-case intrinsic value:** $183  

**Discount to base case:** ($183 − $324.74) ÷ $183 ≈ **−77%** → stock trades at about a **77% premium** to base-case intrinsic value.

- Versus bear ($167): large premium.  
- Versus bull ($280): about **16% premium**.

**Buffett 30% rule:** Price is **not** 30%+ below intrinsic value. It is well above base and bear and above bull. **No margin of safety.**

---

## Verdict

**Hold** — High-quality semiconductor equipment franchise with narrow moat, but current price implies no margin of safety. Base and bear cases suggest material overvaluation; bull case is below the current quote. Cyclicality and customer concentration add risk. For a value-oriented entry, wait for a meaningful discount to base-case intrinsic value (e.g. 30%+ below $183, or ~$128 and below).

---

## Top 3 Permanent Impairment Risks

1. **Semiconductor capex cycle** — Equipment demand is tied to fab spend; a prolonged downcycle or pushouts could compress revenue and margins and permanently reset expectations for growth and returns.  
2. **Customer and geographic concentration** — Large exposure to a handful of customers and to China; export controls, trade policy, or customer consolidation could permanently impair access or pricing.  
3. **Technology and competitive shift** — Share loss to ASML, Lam, or others in key nodes, or a shift in chip architecture that reduces demand for AMAT’s tools, could erode moat and returns.
