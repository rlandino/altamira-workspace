## Moat Assessment

**Rating:** **Wide**

| Source | Score (1–5) | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Brand power** | 4 | Waste Management is the dominant national brand in waste; municipalities and enterprises associate it with reliability and scale. |
| **Switching costs** | 4 | Long-term collection and disposal contracts, route density, and landfill permits create meaningful switching cost for customers. |
| **Network effects** | 3 | Route density improves unit economics; limited classic network effects but scale in collection and disposal matters. |
| **Cost advantages** | 5 | Scale in 255+ landfills, 96 MRFs, 340 transfer stations; integrated collection and disposal; industry-leading margins. |
| **Efficient scale** | 5 | Permitting and zoning make new landfills extremely difficult; incumbent footprint is hard to replicate; natural oligopoly. |

**Summary:** Wide moat from landfill scarcity, route density, and integrated collection-and-disposal scale. Main risks are labor, regulation, and recycling/commodity volatility.

## Margin of Safety

- **Current price:** $246.05  
- **Base-case intrinsic value:** $175  

**Premium to base case:** ($246.05 − $175) ÷ $175 ≈ **41%** → stock trades at about a **41% premium** to base-case intrinsic value.

- Versus bear ($151): current price is **above** bear-case IV.  
- Versus bull ($229): current price is **above** bull-case IV.

**Buffett 30% rule:** Price is **not** 30%+ below base-case intrinsic value. **Margin-of-safety rule is not satisfied** — no margin of safety; stock is materially above base-case IV.

## Verdict

**Avoid** — Wide moat and quality franchise, but current price sits at a large premium (~41%) to base-case intrinsic value with no margin of safety. For strict value entry, wait for a significant pullback toward or below base-case IV.

---

## Top 3 Permanent Impairment Risks

1. **Regulatory and permitting pressure** — Stricter environmental or emissions rules, landfill caps, or permitting delays could permanently constrain capacity and raise compliance cost.  
2. **Labor and wage inflation** — Tight labor market and wage pressure could permanently compress margins in a labor-intensive collection business if not fully passed through.  
3. **Recycling economics and commodity exposure** — Structural decline in recycling commodity prices or policy shifts (e.g. single-stream changes) could permanently impair MRF and recycling profitability.
