## Owner Earnings Calculation

Using **FY 2025** (calendar year ending 2025-12-31) from FMP. All figures in millions USD.

| Component | Amount ($M) | Note |
|-----------|-------------|------|
| Net Income | 1,748 | Income statement FY 2025 |
| + Depreciation & Amortization | 738 | Cash flow statement |
| − Maintenance Capex | 738 | Assumed equal to D&A (total capex $868M; D&A as maintenance proxy for software company) |
| − Working capital increase | 29 | changeInWorkingCapital FY 2025 positive = WC increase (cash use) |
| **Owner Earnings** | **1,719** | |

**Shares outstanding:** 1,046,000,000 (quote).  
**Owner earnings per share:** $1,719M ÷ 1,046M ≈ **$1.64/share**.

*Assumption note:* ServiceNow is a high-growth SaaS company with material stock-based compensation (SBC); GAAP net income understates cash generation. FCF in FY 2025 was $4,576M (~$4.37/share), well above owner earnings as calculated here. This analysis uses the standard owner-earnings formula; growth investors may place more weight on FCF or revenue growth. Maintenance capex is taken as D&A; total capex ($868M) is modest relative to operating cash flow.

## Moat Assessment

**Rating:** **Wide**

| Source | Score (1–5) | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Brand power** | 5 | ServiceNow is the category leader in enterprise workflow, IT service management, and digital transformation; strong brand with CIOs and enterprises. |
| **Switching costs** | 5 | Deep integration into IT and business workflows, custom apps, and data; very high cost to rip and replace. |
| **Network effects** | 4 | Platform ecosystem, App Engine, and partner solutions add value as more customers and apps adopt; strong but not purely viral. |
| **Cost advantages** | 4 | Scale in R&D and go-to-market; high gross margins and operating leverage as revenue scales. |

**Summary:** Wide moat from platform leadership, switching costs, and efficient scale. Main risks are competition, execution, and IT spending cycles.

## Margin of Safety

- **Current price:** $124.34  
- **Base-case intrinsic value:** $36  

**Premium to base case:** ($124.34 − $36) ÷ $36 ≈ **245%** → stock trades at a **very large premium** to base-case intrinsic value derived from current owner earnings.

- Versus bear ($31): current price is **above** bear-case IV.  
- Versus bull ($47): current price is **above** bull-case IV.

**Buffett 30% rule:** Price is **not** 30%+ below base-case intrinsic value. **Margin-of-safety rule is not satisfied** — no margin of safety on an OE-based DCF; the stock is priced for substantial future growth.

---

## Verdict

**Avoid** — Wide moat and category leader, but current price is far above intrinsic value derived from owner earnings. NOW is a growth compounder valued on future FCF and expansion; strict value discipline requires a large margin of safety that is not present. For growth-oriented investors, FCF-based or revenue-multiple frameworks may be more relevant.

---

## Top 3 Permanent Impairment Risks

1. **Competitive displacement** — A major cloud or enterprise vendor (e.g. Microsoft, Salesforce) could invest heavily in workflow/ITSM and permanently take share or compress pricing in core markets.  
2. **IT budget and macro pressure** — Sustained cuts in enterprise IT spending or a prolonged downturn could permanently slow deal flow and expansion, reducing growth and terminal value.  
3. **Execution and dilution** — Failure to sustain platform innovation or excessive SBC/dilution could permanently impair per-share economics and investor returns.
