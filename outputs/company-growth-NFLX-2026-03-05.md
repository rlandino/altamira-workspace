## Optional metrics

- **Net income growth:** 3yr CAGR 34.6%; latest YoY 26.1%.
- **Gross margin:** 48.49% (latest); up from 46.06% (FY24) and 39.37% (FY22).
- **EBITDA margin:** 66.96% (latest); up from 67.47% (FY24) and 64.31% (FY22); high and stable.
- **ROE:** 41.26% (latest); up from 35.21% (FY24) and 21.62% (FY22).
- **FCF (dollars) growth:** 3yr CAGR 79.5%; latest YoY 36.7%.
- **Rule of 40:** Revenue growth 15.85% + Operating margin 29.49% = **45.3** → **Elite** (≥40).
- **Revenue growth trajectory:** **Accelerating** — FY22 6.5%, FY23 6.7%, FY24 15.6%, FY25 15.9%; reacceleration from 2024.
- **PEG ratio:** P/E ~36 ÷ ~27% (EPS YoY) ≈ 1.3; ratios show priceEarningsToGrowthRatio ~1.33.
- **Asset turnover:** 0.81 (latest); improved from 0.65 (FY22) as revenue grew faster than assets.

## Metrics to monitor

- **Revenue and subscriber growth:** Paid membership, ARPU, and geographic mix; advertising tier contribution.
- **Content spend and FCF:** FCF margin sustainability as content investment and licensing evolve; balance of originals vs licensed.
- **Operating leverage:** Path to mid-30s operating margin; competition and pricing power.
- **ROIC sustainability:** 25% ROIC vs cost of capital; incremental returns on content and tech investment.
- **EPS and buybacks:** Share count decline and margin expansion driving EPS; consistency vs consensus.
- **Competition and churn:** Disney+, Max, Amazon Prime; retention and win-back initiatives.
