## Moat Assessment

**Rating:** **Wide**

| Source | Score (1–5) | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Brand power** | 5 | Apple is one of the world’s strongest brands; premium pricing and loyalty across iPhone, Mac, and services. |
| **Switching costs** | 5 | iOS/macOS ecosystem, iCloud, App Store, and device integration create high switching cost for users and developers. |
| **Network effects** | 5 | App Store, developer and accessory ecosystems, and installed base reinforce platform value. |
| **Cost advantages** | 4 | Scale in components and supply chain; less durable than software-only moats given hardware competition. |
| **Efficient scale** | 5 | Few players can match global hardware-plus-services scale and ecosystem; high ROIC. |

**Summary:** Wide moat from brand, ecosystem lock-in, and scale. Main risks are iPhone concentration, China, and hardware cycle.

## Intrinsic Value Range (10-year DCF)

Base-year owner earnings: **$9.32/share**.  
10-year explicit projection, then terminal value. Terminal growth: 2% (bear), 3% (base), 4% (bull).

| Scenario | Growth (yr 1–10) | Discount rate | Terminal g | Intrinsic value (per share) |
|----------|------------------|---------------|------------|-----------------------------|
| Bear     | 3%               | 8%            | 2%         | **$190**                    |
| Base     | 8%               | 10%           | 3%         | **$364**                    |
| Bull     | 15%              | 12%           | 4%         | **$253**                    |

**Range:** $190 bear / $364 base / $253 bull (per share).

*Note:* Bull-case IV is below base because 15% growth with 12% discount shifts value toward the 10-year period; terminal value is sensitive to terminal g. Base case is the primary reference for margin of safety.

*Assumptions:* Owner earnings $9.32/share; 10-year growth and discount rates as in table; terminal value = FCF year 11 × (1 + g_term) / (r − g_term); share count ~14.70B.

## Margin of Safety

- **Current price:** $257.46  
- **Base-case intrinsic value:** $364  

**Discount to base case:** ($364 − $257.46) ÷ $364 ≈ **29%** → stock trades at about a **29% discount** to base-case intrinsic value.

- Versus bear ($190): current price is **above** bear-case IV.  
- Versus bull ($253): current price is **slightly above** bull-case IV.

**Buffett 30% rule:** Price is **not** 30% or more below base-case intrinsic value (29% discount). **Margin-of-safety rule is not satisfied** for a strict buy on base case alone.

---

## Verdict

**Hold** — Wide-moat, high-quality franchise trading at a meaningful but sub‑30% discount to base-case intrinsic value. At a slightly lower price (e.g. ~$255 or below for a 30% margin to $364), the margin-of-safety rule would be met; at current levels, Hold is appropriate for a strict value discipline.

---

## Top 3 Permanent Impairment Risks

1. **iPhone dependency and smartphone saturation** — A sustained shift away from the smartphone upgrade cycle, or share loss in key regions (e.g. China), could permanently reduce hardware revenue and profit; Services depend on the installed base.  
2. **China regulatory and supply-chain concentration** — Regulatory pressure, local competition, or supply-chain disruption (e.g. Taiwan/Asia) could permanently impair revenue, margins, or production.  
3. **Leadership and innovation succession** — Post-Cook succession or a prolonged period without a major new product category could erode premium positioning and investor confidence in long-term growth.
