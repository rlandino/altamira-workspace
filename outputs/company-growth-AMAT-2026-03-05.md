## Grade

**Grade: D.**

**Reason for grade:** Revenue growth is modest (3yr CAGR 3.2%, latest YoY 4.4%)—in the D band (0–5%). EPS growth is low (3yr CAGR 5.3%, latest YoY 0.6%)—C band. FCF margin remains high in level (20.1%) but declined sharply YoY (−7.5 pp) on a step-up in CapEx and lower operating cash flow, so the trend is deteriorating (D). ROIC remains strong in level (22.0%) but has declined materially from prior years and is down 14.9% YoY (D for declining). Two core metrics in D (revenue, FCF margin trend, ROIC trend) and modest EPS growth drive a composite grade of D.

## Metrics to monitor

- **Revenue and cycle:** Semiconductor equipment demand is cyclical; monitor order trends, WFE outlook, and China exposure for revenue re-acceleration or further deceleration.
- **FCF margin and CapEx:** FY25 FCF margin dropped on materially higher CapEx ($2.26B vs $1.19B); watch whether CapEx normalizes and FCF margin stabilizes or recovers.
- **ROIC sustainability:** ROIC has declined from peak (34%+ in FY22) to 22%; track that incremental investments earn above cost of capital.
- **EPS vs consensus:** FY25 EPS flat YoY (8.66 vs 8.61); consensus implies meaningful growth in FY26—monitor execution and buybacks.
- **Rule of 40:** Currently Solid (33.6); revenue growth is the main lever in a cyclical recovery.
- **Tax and other income:** FY25 net income was pressured by higher effective tax rate and lower other income; monitor for normalization.
