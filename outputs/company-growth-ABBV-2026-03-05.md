## Grade

**Grade: F.**

**Reason for grade:** Revenue growth on a 3yr basis is weak (CAGR 1.8%)—D band. EPS has collapsed (3yr CAGR −43%, latest YoY −1.3%) as Humira erosion, acquisition-related amortization, and restructuring compressed net income—F. FCF margin remains high in level (29.1%) but has declined materially over three years and YoY (−2.5 pp)—D for declining. ROIC at 13.7% is in the C band (≥10%) and improved sharply YoY from a depressed FY24. Per the worst-of rule, EPS in the F band caps the composite grade at F.

## Metrics to monitor

- **EPS and earnings quality:** EPS collapse reflects Humira decline, amortization, and one-time items; watch ex-GAAP earnings, pipeline, and SKYRIZI/RINVOQ growth for sustainable recovery.
- **FCF margin sustainability:** FCF margin remains strong (29%) but has stepped down; monitor that dividend and debt service remain covered as portfolio shifts.
- **ROIC vs cost of capital:** ROIC recovered to 13.7% YoY; track that incremental R&D and M&A earn above cost of capital.
- **Revenue re-acceleration:** Revenue growth improved to 8.6% YoY; watch immunology and aesthetics for durability vs consensus.
- **Rule of 40:** Currently Elite (43.3); operating margin expansion and revenue growth are the two levers to maintain tier.
- **Patent and pipeline:** Humira biosimilar pressure and pipeline readouts drive long-term revenue and EPS trajectory.

## Caveats

- **Data source:** FMP API v3 (income statement, cash flow, key-metrics, ratios, financial-growth, annual, limit 5). Quote and profile for context.
- **EPS:** FMP provides GAAP diluted EPS; report uses **EPS (GAAP)**. GAAP EPS is heavily impacted by amortization of intangibles from acquisitions (Allergan, Cerevel, etc.) and restructuring; adjusted/non-GAAP EPS is typically higher—see company filings.
- **FCF margin:** Computed as (operatingCashFlow − capitalExpenditure) / revenue. FCF remains robust and dividend is well covered; margin decline reflects revenue mix and OCF volatility.
- **ROIC:** From key-metrics `roic` (decimal). FY24 ROIC was depressed (8.85%); FY25 recovery reflects higher operating income and capital structure changes.
- **Negative equity:** FY25 stockholders’ equity is negative (retained deficit, buybacks); ROE and some ratios are distorted.
