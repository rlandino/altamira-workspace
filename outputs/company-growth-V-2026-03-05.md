- **PEG ratio (approx.):** P/E ~33 ÷ ~10% (EPS 3yr CAGR) ≈ 3.3; premium valuation vs growth.

## Metrics to monitor

- **Revenue durability:** Volume and pricing in payments; cross-border and value-added services mix; competition from other networks and real-time rails.
- **Operating margin normalization:** FY25 margin step-down (litigation/other); watch for sustainability of 60%+ operating margin.
- **ROIC vs cost of capital:** ROIC ~28% is well above cost of capital; monitor incremental returns on technology and M&A spend.
- **EPS consistency vs consensus:** FY25 EPS growth slowed YoY (4.8%); share repurchases and margin path drive future EPS.
- **Rule of 40:** Elite tier (71.3); any material drop in revenue growth or margin would still likely keep Elite.
- **Regulation and interchange:** Regulatory and legal outcomes (e.g. interchange, antitrust) that could affect revenue or margins.
