## Grade

**Grade: B.**

**Reason for grade:** Revenue growth is solid (3yr CAGR 12.4%, latest YoY 14.9%)—in the B band (10–15%). EPS growth is similarly strong (3yr CAGR 12.3%, YoY 15.6%)—also B. FCF margin remains high at 25.4% and well above 15%, but has declined YoY (−4.8 pp) and over three years as CapEx has stepped up sharply (cloud/datacenter build); level supports B. ROIC at 22.0% is above 20% and above cost of capital, with a modest YoY decline—B. All four core metrics are in the B band: no A-level expansion on margin or ROIC, but no material weakness; composite grade is B.

## Metrics to monitor

- **FCF margin and CapEx:** FCF margin stepped down to 25.4% on much higher CapEx ($64.6B in FY25 vs $44.5B FY24); monitor whether CapEx intensity moderates and FCF margin stabilizes or recovers.
- **ROIC vs invested capital:** ROIC remains strong (22%) but has drifted down as invested capital grew; track that incremental investments earn above cost of capital.
- **Revenue deceleration risk:** Growth re-accelerated to ~15% YoY; watch Azure, Office/Teams, and Windows commercial for any slowdown vs consensus.
- **EPS consistency vs consensus:** Strong recent EPS growth; monitor execution and buybacks vs elevated expectations.
- **Rule of 40 sustainability:** Currently Elite (60.6); operating margin and revenue growth are the two levers to watch.
- **AI/cloud capital intensity:** Ongoing build-out for AI and Azure may keep CapEx elevated; balance growth and FCF conversion.
