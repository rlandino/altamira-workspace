## Metrics to monitor

- **Revenue reacceleration:** FY25 revenue growth 7.9% vs 13.7% in FY24; sustainability of mid-single-digit growth and any reacceleration in Ratings, Indices, and Market Intelligence.
- **ROIC path above 10%:** ROIC 9.2% is just below the 10% band; incremental returns on invested capital and deleveraging.
- **FCF margin sustainability:** High margin (35.6%) with YoY decline; watch OCF and CapEx as share of revenue.
- **IHS merger integration:** Synergies, cost saves, and segment mix; FY22–23 margins were depressed by integration.
- **EPS consistency:** Strong EPS growth and buybacks; dilution and margin trajectory.
- **Capital allocation:** Debt paydown, buybacks, and M&A; balance sheet and leverage.
