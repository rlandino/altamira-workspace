---

## Grade

**Grade: B.**

**Reason for grade:** Revenue growth is solid but in the 5–10% band (3yr CAGR 6.6%, latest YoY 8.2%). EPS growth is stronger (3yr CAGR 11.5%, YoY 10%), supporting a B. FCF margin is low in level (2.85%) but has expanded from 1.54% three years ago and is up YoY; that supports a C for level with an improving trend. ROIC is high (19.4%) and above cost of capital, but down slightly YoY. On balance, the core four point to good quality growth with no major red flags—hence B.

## Metrics to monitor

- **Revenue deceleration risk:** 3yr revenue CAGR ~6.6%; watch quarterly comps and membership growth for further deceleration or stability.
- **FCF margin sustainability:** FCF margin remains modest (2.85%) despite expansion; monitor CapEx intensity and working capital as warehouse growth continues.
- **ROIC vs cost of capital:** ROIC 19.4% is strong; track that it stays well above cost of capital as the base grows.
- **EPS consistency vs consensus:** Recent double beats (e.g. Q2 FY26); monitor execution vs elevated expectations.
- **Rule of 40 / margin mix:** Rule of 40 is weak given low operating margin; for context only—retail model differs from software.
- **SG&A and wage pressure:** Operating margin improved slightly; watch healthcare and wage costs (as noted in earnings calls) for leverage.
