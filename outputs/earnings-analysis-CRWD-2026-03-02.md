## 5. Earnings call — prepared remarks highlights

- **Record Q3:** Net new ARR $265M (73% y/y), beating expectations by double-digit millions; ending ARR $4.92B (23% y/y). Record Q3 free cash flow $296M (24% of revenue); record operating income $265M (21% of revenue).
- **Platform acceleration:** Broad-based ARR acceleration across cloud, next-gen identity, and next-gen SIEM; endpoint business also accelerated. Falcon Flex ending ARR >$1.35B (>200% y/y).
- **Guidance raised:** FY26 revenue guide raised by $24.1M at midpoint to $4.797–$4.807B (21–22% y/y). Q4 revenue $1.29–$1.30B; non-GAAP EPS Q4 ~$1.09–$1.11; FY26 EPS $3.70–$3.72.
- **Strategic themes:** AI as largest opportunity; “agentic” workforce expanding attack surface; single-platform (Falcon) as “operating system” for cybersecurity; Charlotte AI (agentic SOC); NextGen SIEM as scale disruptor; AWS partnership (NextGen SIEM in AWS Security Hub).
- **Notable deals:** Large European bank (8-figure) with NextGen SIEM, Onum, Charlotte; Kroll MSSP partnership (nearly 500k endpoints migrating to Falcon); F5 partnership (Falcon on BIG-IP); Fortune 500 wins in cloud (Wiz displacement), Falcon Shield, federal.
- **Cash / P&L:** Cash $4.8B; GAAP net loss $34M (includes ~$26M incident-related, ~$5.6M acquisition-related); non-GAAP net income $245.4M ($0.96/share). Q4 FCF margin guide 27%; FY26 FCF margin 25%.

## 7. Sentiment analysis (management answers)

- **Tone:** **Confident.** Repeated “fantastic,” “all-star,” “record,” and conviction on platform consolidation, AI opportunity, and single-platform differentiation. No defensive or apologetic tone; forward commitment on FY26/FY27 and pipeline.
- **Sentiment:** **Positive.** Optimism on NextGen SIEM, Flex, cloud displacements, AWS, and endpoint acceleration. Cautious only on normal-course discounting and “more to do” on identity/observability; no negative guidance or tone.
- **Clarity:** **Clear and specific.** Numerical guidance (Q4 revenue, EPS, FY26 ranges, FCF margins), named deals (Kroll, F5, European bank), and product-level color. Burt deferred segment/NRR-GRR to year-end; no evasive dodges on material topics.
- **Notable Q&A themes:** (1) **NextGen SIEM and consolidation** — Displacement of legacy SIEM and Splunk; disruptive pricing; multiyear wallet-share expansion. (2) **Falcon Flex and NRR** — Flex as continuous net ARR driver; bigger and longer deals; consolidation and TCO. (3) **AI and endpoint** — AI at the endpoint as catalyst; Charlotte and agents beyond chatbots; “operating system for the SOC.” (4) **Partnerships** — AWS, F5, Kroll, and hyperscaler/multi-cloud positioning.
